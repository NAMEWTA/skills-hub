create, update, complete, or delete .agents/skills-hub-licenses/ there.

they must not assume the installer has provided a legal bundle.

not replace licenses, notices, or third-party rights associated with copied,
translated, adapted, or passively retained source material. Original legal
files also remain in their original locations within source snapshots.
